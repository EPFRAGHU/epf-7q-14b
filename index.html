    How the amounts are calculated
    • Due date is the 15th of the month following the wage month. Delay is counted in days from the due date to the date of payment.
    • A/c 1 is the employer's share less 8.33% pension, plus the employees' share unless exempt. The PF rate is 10% for wage months up to Sep-1997 and 12% from Oct-1997 (raised from 22.09.1997), unless 10% throughout is selected. With 12%: A/c 1 is 15.67%, or 3.67% if the employees' share is exempt.
    • Other rates on wages: A/c 2 at 0.65% up to Jul-1998, 1.10% from Aug-1998 to Dec-2014, 0.85% from Jan-2015, 0.65% from Apr-2017 and 0.50% from Jun-2018; A/c 10 at 8.33%; A/c 21 at 0.50%; A/c 22 at 0.01% up to Mar-2017 and nil after.
    • 7Q interest is 12% a year on each account's contribution for the days of delay, rounded to the rupee. It applies to dues falling on or after 01.07.1997, when Section 7Q came into force.
    • 14B for months due before 14.06.2024 uses a per-annum rate for the days of delay, set by the total period of delay. For dues before 26.09.2008 the Para 32A slabs are 17%, 22%, 27% and 37% (under 2, 2–4, 4–6, and 6 months or more); from 26.09.2008 they are 5%, 10%, 15% and 25%. The highest-slab option applies 37% or 25% to every month, as in the Excel sheet. For months due from 14.06.2024 it is 1% of the contribution for each month of delay, with any part of a month counted as a full month (GSR 327(E) dated 14.06.2024; EPFO letter dated 28.08.2024).
    • For each year, 14B levied (D) is limited to 100% of the contribution (A). Amount to be deposited is (B) + (D).
